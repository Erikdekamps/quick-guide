  • The Essence of Magic — Have Wizard Cromperty teleport you to the essence mine.
    How to

    Talk to Wizard Cromperty and have him teleport the player to the Rune Essence mine.

  • Yoink! — Attempt to steal from any stall in Ardougne Market, or from any guard.
    Needs 5 Thieving ✓
    How to

    Attempt to steal from any stall in Ardougne market, or from any guard.

  • Silky Smooth — Sell silk to the silk trader in Ardougne for 60gp each by haggling.
    How to

    Sell silk to the silk merchant in Ardougne for 60 coins each.
    It requires talking to the merchant and offering a price of 120 first, then responding to his counter offer with a price of 60.

  • Preaching to the Infected — Use the altar in West Ardougne's church.
    Needs Plague City
    How to

    Use the altar in West Ardougne's church.

  • Playing the Waiting Game — Enter the Castle Wars waiting room.
    How to

    Enter the Castle Wars waiting room. Just deposit all your non-combat items in the bank chest and enter any portal. You can exit the room using the portal in the centre.

  • Gone Fishing — Go out fishing on the Fishing Trawler.
    How to

    Go out fishing on the Fishing Trawler.
    The player does not need to complete a trip on the Trawler; the fishbowl helmet and diving apparatus are not needed; pets (such as a cat) are not allowed, including in the backpack.

  • Boot Camp — Enter the Combat Training Camp north of West Ardougne.
    Needs Biohazard
    How to

    Enter the Combat Training Camp north of West Ardougne.

  • A Cat is For Life... — Talk to a civilian in West Ardougne about selling a cat.
    Needs Plague City, Gertrude's Cat
    How to

    Talk to a civilian in West Ardougne about selling a cat while having a non-kitten cat in the inventory. Civilians are located in the north-western area inside West Ardougne. The cat does not need to be sold to complete this achievement.
    Using a cat on the civilian will result in the cat being sold, while also completing the achievement.
    Cats that can be used for this achievement are:
    • Purple cats
    • Hellcats
    • Fully grown cats
    • Overgrown cats
    • Wily cats
    • Lazy cats

  • Creator and Destroyer — Kill a unicow in the Tower of Life.
    Needs 10 Construction, Tower of Life
    How to

    Kill a unicow in the Tower of Life.
    To make a unicow, a cowhide and a unicorn horn need to be used on the north-western altar in the basement of the Tower of Life. Players need to have finished the Tower of Life quest to get into the tower's basement.

  • Red Revolution — Get a civilian to throw a tomato at the army recruiter in West Ardougne.
    Needs Plague City
    How to

    Get a civilian to throw a tomato at the recruiter in the West Ardougne town square. The player must talk to the recruiter and go through the dialogue, which will eventually lead the civilians to throw tomatoes at him.

  • Going on a Summer Holiday — Sail from East Ardougne to Karamja.
    How to

    Sail from East Ardougne to Karamja by talking to Captain Barnaby on the dock just east of The Poison Arrow in south-eastern East Ardougne costing 30 coins (free if the ring of charos is worn).

  • Breaking and Entering — Attempt to picklock a certain door east of the gem stall in Ardougne's marketplace.
    Needs 16 Thieving ✓
    How to

    Attempt to pick-lock the door east of the gem stall in Ardougne Market which is the southern house on the east side of the marketplace. Right click on the door and select "Pick-lock". The attempt does not need to be successful for the achievement to be completed.

  • P-P-P-Pick Up Some Prizes — Spend some Penguin Points with Larry (or Chuck) at Ardougne Zoo.
    How to

    Spend some penguin points with Larry (or Chuck) at Ardougne Zoo.

  • A Gift from Khazard — Use the Summoning obelisk to the east of the Fight Arena.
    How to

    Use the Summoning obelisk to the east of the Fight Arena. This can even be done with full Summoning points.

  • Party Pooper — Pop a balloon in the monastery south of Ardougne.
    Needs Monk's Friend
    How to

    Pop a balloon in the Ardougne Monastery south of Ardougne. Talk to Brother Omad to make some balloons drop.
    The quest does not have to be completed in order to complete this achievement; the balloon drop can be triggered by a different player.

  • Vial Deeds — Buy a water-filled vial from the general store run by Aemad and Kortan.
    How to

    Buy a vial of water costing 10 coins from Aemad's Adventuring Supplies which is run by Aemad and Kortan and is located south of the church and north of the Ardougne Zoo.

  • Star Seeker — Use the noticeboard near the Observatory to gather information about shooting stars.
    How to

    Use the Shooting Star noticeboard near the Observatory to gather information about shooting stars. The noticeboard is not in the Observatory but in the small house where the Observatory Quest is started, just north of the Observatory.
    The players can also find the Shooting Star noticeboard next to Wizard Rosalina on the 2nd floor (UK) / 3rd floor (US) of the Wizards' Tower, which allows free-to-play players to do this task.

  • Dukes of Khazard — Kill something on the Khazard Battlefield.
    How to

    Kill something on the Khazard Battlefield. The battlefield is north of the Tree Gnome Village. Killing a combatant on any side will work.

  • Don't Eat the Pointy Bit — Buy a skewered kebab from the Poison Arrow pub.
    How to

    Buy a skewered kebab from The Poison Arrow pub which costs 25 coins. The Poison Arrow pub is located in south-east East Ardougne, just south-east of the bank.

  • Bargain Hunter — View the Hunter equipment in Aleck's shop in Yanille.
    How to

    View the Hunter equipment in Aleck's Hunter Emporium. Aleck and his shop are located in south-west Yanille.

  • Are You Being Served? — Talk to the chief servant at the servants' guild to find out about servants for your house.
    How to

    Talk to the Chief Servant at the Servants' Guild to find out about servants for a player-owned house. The Servants' Guild is located in the north-eastern corner of East Ardougne, just south-east of the Ardougne lodestone.

  • No Time to Lose — Use a ring of duelling to teleport to Castle Wars.
    Needs 27 Crafting, 27 Magic ✓, The following are not required if an emerald ring or ring of duelling are obtained as a drop instead:, One of:• 33 Magic Magic or• 40 Smithing Smithing, The following is not required if a ring of duelling is obtained as a drop:
    How to

    Use a ring of duelling to teleport to Castle Wars by choosing the Castle Wars option on the ring. This achievement can be completed by using a stored ring of duelling within a passage of the abyss.

  • They're Long and Pointy — Talk to Tindel Marchant about identifying swords on Kandarin's east coast.
    How to

    Talk to Tindel Marchant about identifying swords. He is located at the end of the southern most pier in eastern Port Khazard. Merely asking him about swords is enough to complete the achievement, so a rusty sword is not required.
