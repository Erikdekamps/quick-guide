  • Everything Is Oresome — Increase the capacity of your ore box by mining 100 of each of the listed ores.
    Needs 90 Mining (boostable)
    Mine 100 of each of the ores listed below. This includes mining light and dark animica rocks, which requires level 90 Mining. Completing the achievement increases the capacity of all ore boxes by 20 per ore (excluding platinum, gold and silver ore). Once the achievement is unlocked, the extra ore space can be used on free worlds, although membership is required to mine higher than runite ore.

  • It Should Have Been Called Aetherium — Unlock the ability to smith masterwork armour by making and upgrading to +5 a full set of elder rune armour.
    Needs 90 Smithing
