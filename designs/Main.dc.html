  • Search the table.

Feldip Hunter area

  • Talk to the Hunting expert in the Feldip Hunter area about tracking a person.
  • Trap the square trap pit to the west, then use the mort myre fungus on it.
  • Dismantle the collapsed trap to get an unconscious broav.
  • Talk to the Hunting expert again about tracking a person.

Khazard

Map: Location of the Khazard launderer

The wiki’s map

Location of the Khazard launderer · Open on the wiki

Dismiss any followers before the next steps.
  • Go to the laundry room west of the Fight Arena, two houses north of the pub.
  • With the ring of charos (a) equipped, talk to the Khazard launderer to get dirty laundry.
    • 2Do you know someone called Movario?
    • 2(Charm) Tell me all you know about Movario.

    Brought coins instead? Take them out of your coin pouch and use them on the launderer:

    • 2No, I need information. Do you know someone called Movario?
    • 1Offer five thousand coins for information on Movario.
  • Go north-west to the Khazard Battleground, north of Tree Gnome Village across the bridges.
  • Climb-over the crumbled wall in the north-westernmost building.
  • Drop the broav in the room with the broken table, then use the dirty laundry on it.
  • Search the broken table to find a trapdoor, then go down.

Old battered door

  • Pick-up the broav.
  • Go through the door, follow the path north and climb-down the stairs.
  • Search the old battered door and use the right rune on it. You hear a click when it is right.
  • Search the door again and disarm the trap. Boost Thieving if you need to.
    • 1Yes, I'm an awesome thief, I'll disable it easily!
  • Go through the old battered door.

“Prohibited” is the key word. In-game only one of its letters is swapped for a symbol. Match that symbol to a rune.

Inside Movario's base

  • Search the painting on the east wall.
    • 1Yes, I'll flip the catch and see what happens.
  • Search the bookcases until the chat says “CLICK!”.
  • Follow the faintly glowing wire to the next bookcase. Zoom in if the glow is hard to see.
    • Search that bookcase.
    • Repeat until the electricity on the gate is gone.
  • Open the gate, search the spiral staircase to disarm it, then climb-up.
  • Search the desk for Movario's notes (volume 1).
    • Read the notes.
  • Pick-up the waste-paper basket.
    • With the notes in your backpack, search the basket.
  • Search the bookcase west of the basket.
    • Use the ruby key on the bookcase.
  • Climb-up the stairs just north of you.
  • Search the bed.
    • Use the ruby key on the bed chest.
  • Search the bed chest to disarm its traps. Retry until it works.
    • 1Yes, I'll attempt it.
  • Use the ruby key on the chest, then open and search it for the strange key loop, strange key teeth and Movario's notes (volume 2). Read them.
  • Climb down twice to the wire room, cross-over the broken wall and search the drawers.
  • Look-at the thermometer and note the number. Compare it with your weight in the equipment screen.
Caution: items dropped in this room are lost.
  • Take weights from the pile on the floor to match that difference.
  • Climb back up. Heavier than the number: use the weights on the statue just north, then open the door east.
Done right, opening the door gives “The magical interference to teleportation has lifted.”
  • Go east through the door and up the ladder to leave the hideout.
